# Tyohar — Play Console listing (draft)

**Title:** Tyohar: Puja, Prasad & Temple Offerings

**Short description:** Book puja & hawan, send bhet, or we collect your offering and submit it at temples across India.

**Full description:**

Tyohar is for anyone who is busy, travelling, or living away from home and still wants to offer prayer at a temple in India.

• Book puja or hawan online in your name and gotra  
• Offer coconut, til, oil, chunri, and other bhet at famous temples  
• Schedule a pickup — we collect homemade prasad or offerings from your door and submit them at the temple you choose  
• Receive photos of the seva and prasad packed back to your home  

This first Play Store release includes a complete booking journey. Live UPI payments, pan-India rider coverage, and WhatsApp updates will follow.
