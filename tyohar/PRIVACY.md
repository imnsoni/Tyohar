# Privacy (Tyohar v1)

Tyohar version 1 stores your name, phone, gotra, address, family members, cart, and bookings **on this device only** (AsyncStorage). Nothing is uploaded to a Tyohar server in this build.

When live payments and operations go live, this policy will be updated to cover:

- Account and order data needed to perform puja, pickup, and prasad delivery
- Payment processor data (UPI / cards) handled by the payment partner
- Photos or videos of sevas shared with you
